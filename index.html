The Sticky Seed Reader

Install or update your Seeed Studio reTerminal Sticky (ESP32-S3 32MB) directly from your browser.

  • Multi-Format Reader: EPUB 2/3, PDF Reflow, MOBI, TXT, CBZ Manga, FB2
  • Wireless Sideloading: Drag & drop web portal + Standard Ebooks & Calibre
  • Smart Standby: Magnetic ambient clock with 15-min auto power save
  • Safe Dual OTA: Dual 6.5MB partitions with automatic bootloader rollback
WebSerial is not supported in this browser. Please use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on desktop over HTTPS.
⚠️ Connection & Bootloader Tip:
ESP32-S3 boards require entering the ROM Bootloader mode for initial flashing:
1. Unplug the USB-C cable from your computer.
2. Press and hold the center [OK / BOOT] button on your reTerminal Sticky.
3. While holding the button, plug the USB-C cable back in.
4. Release the button, then click CONNECT & INSTALL FIRMWARE above.
